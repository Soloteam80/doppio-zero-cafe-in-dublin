Our Story

Doppio Zero: A Taste of Italy in Dublin

Located on Abbey Street in Dublin city centre, Doppio Zero has been serving authentic Italian food since 2013. From lunchtime panini to pizzas and pasta dishes, everything is prepared with care and tradition.

Offering a warm, welcoming atmosphere, it’s a place to enjoy simple, honest Italian flavours and take a moment to slow down and savour good food the Italian way daily.
